---
title: swiyu Wallet - Increasing the minimal version to Android 12 
---

With the upcoming swiyu (Sandbox) Wallet version 1.17.0 for Android, only devices running Android 12 or higher will be supported.

Android 10 & 11 have not received any security updates for some time and do not support a security feature we have implemented.

The new version is expected to be released on August 20 or 24.

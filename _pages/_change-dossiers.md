Change Dossiers summarize major changes with [different components](https://swiyu-admin-ch.github.io/open-source-components/#version-overview) involved. They show the necessary steps for the migration and a timeline, when these steps need to be taken. The information about new Change Dossiers is provided via the [release announcements](https://swiyu-admin-ch.github.io/release-announcements/).
